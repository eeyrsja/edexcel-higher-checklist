How Paper 1 is marked

In the current format you have 1 hour 45 minutes for two essays. Use the printed extract and examples from across the play or novel.

  • AO1 · Make an argument and support it with references.
  • AO2 · Explain how the writer uses language, form and structure.
  • AO3 · Use context that helps explain your reading of the text.
  • AO4 · Write clearly and accurately. Macbeth carries four marks for this.
