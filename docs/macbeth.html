Plot at a glance

  1. Act 1 · The witches predict Macbeth’s rise. Duncan names Malcolm heir. Lady Macbeth persuades Macbeth to go through with the murder.
  2. Act 2 · Macbeth kills Duncan and is shaken by what he has done. Duncan’s sons flee and Macbeth becomes king.
  3. Act 3 · Macbeth arranges Banquo’s murder. Fleance escapes. Banquo’s ghost disrupts the banquet.
  4. Act 4 · The apparitions give Macbeth confidence. He orders the killing of Macduff’s family while Macduff joins Malcolm.
  5. Act 5 · Lady Macbeth sleepwalks and dies. Malcolm’s army advances using branches from Birnam Wood. Macduff kills Macbeth.
